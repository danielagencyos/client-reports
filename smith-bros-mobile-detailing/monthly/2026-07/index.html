SMS is doing the heavy lifting: its click rate improved again and remains many times email's, which is typical for a local service business — texts get read. Total clicks eased because the back half of July moved from one big weekly blast to smaller, targeted test sends — the A/B structure we set up to learn which offers convert before scaling them. Subscriber count is reported here for the first time, so it shows as a new baseline rather than a change.

On revenue attribution: Email & SMS are reported here by reach and engagement, not dollars. Tying revenue back to individual campaigns needs the booking data in the warehouse — that groundwork is in progress. UTMs are already on campaign links, so the attribution history is building now.
